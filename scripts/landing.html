Tabs to Trails Code on GitHub

Turn your reading backlog into a walk.

Paste the article you keep meaning to read and say how long your walk is. Your own computer turns it into an MP3 of that length, with Gemma 4 and the Kokoro voice running locally. Scan the code, pocket the phone, go.

Hear two samples Get it on GitHub

About a minute at the screen

  1. 1

    Paste

    A link, some text or a .md file, or up to eight of them, read one after another. Things you already meant to read.

  2. 2

    Pick a length

    10 to 60 minutes, or the whole thing. The plan uses the voice's measured pace.

  3. 3

    Pocket it

    Scan the QR code, download the MP3, press play. A chime and a cue tell you when you're halfway.

Everything runs on your computer

Gemma 4 through OllamaRewrites for the ear, describes code and tables, writes the closing question. A number guard checks every number against the source.
Kokoro-82MReads the script on the CPU. The app measures the audio and shows the real length.
Stays on your machineThe server fetches the links you typed and nothing else. Pasted text works with the internet off.
Pocket your phone.

The MP3 holds the whole walk. Once it's on the phone, the app, the Wi-Fi and the laptop can stay home. Quick start on GitHub.
